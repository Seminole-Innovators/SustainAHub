Protecting the Environment
by connecting humans to it

Our Patch was founded from the heart, with the purpose of creating a community to support sustainability.

Rooted in the Tallahassee Metropolitan area, this project focuses on interacting with our community through local organizations. Tallahassee is known for its vibrant college town, and by introducing these resources to our residents and students alike, we can make a difference in our city. By working together we can learn how to better contribute and connect to sustainability and environmental protection efforts.

We see the world like a big, beautiful garden, and for us, Tallahassee is our patch.
Let's all care for it together.
